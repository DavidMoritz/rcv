About

It is my belief that RCV offers several benefits:

  • Better representation
  • No “Settling”
  • No wasted votes
  • Vote by preference
  • Great for multi-seat elections
  • Easy to use

Proponents of Ranked Choice voting believe this system is about representing the people in the best way possible. The key is allowing the voter to choose more than just their favorite candidate. The problem with only voting for one choice, is that if that choice does not come in first or second, it could be considered a “wasted vote.” Therefore, people are more likely to vote for their second or third choice on the idea that it has a higher chance of winning and it’s better than their last choice.

With Ranked Choice Voting, there is no issue with voting for your first choice first, second choice second, and third choice third. Because if your first choice doesn’t win, then your vote automatically gets transferred to your second choice! And that is the beauty of the system.

Another thing RCV works really well for is implementing a fair method in voting for more multiple positions at once. Everyone’s vote is still counted as one vote, but after their first choice is elected, the unused portion of their vote (equal to the amount not absolutely necessary to get their 1st choice elected) goes to their second choice candidate. Iframe URL on Voting Page

This optional field lets you embed custom content (e.g. an image, custom instructions, company promotional material, etc.) on the voting page, replacing the default "Order by Preference / Drag & Drop" headings. The candidate list and voting buttons remain below.

Sizing the iframe

The iframe is set to 100% width. Prefer a white background for continuity. To control the height, add a small script to your hosted page that sends its height to the parent window: 

<script>
  window.addEventListener('load', function() {
    parent.postMessage(
      { iframeHeight: document.body.scrollHeight },
      '*'
    );
  });
</script>

Ranked Choices will automatically resize the iframe to match.

Requirements

  • The URL must use https://
  • The hosted page must allow embedding (no X-Frame-Options: DENY header)

One Device, One Vote

When enabled, this feature uses browser fingerprinting to detect if the same device has already voted on this ballot. No personal data is collected — only an anonymous device identifier is generated and stored.

Kiosk Mode

If you are using a shared device (e.g. a tablet at an event), log in with your account on that device. As the ballot creator, your device will be automatically whitelisted, allowing multiple people to vote from the same device.

Limitations

  • Different browsers or incognito/private windows on the same device may be treated as separate devices.
  • If a voter's browser blocks fingerprinting, the check is skipped and they can vote normally.
