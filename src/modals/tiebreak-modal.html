Tie-Break Methods

When two or more candidates are tied with the fewest votes in a round, a tie-break rule decides which candidate is eliminated.

Random (official)

One of the tied candidates is eliminated at random. This is the standard method used in official ranked choice elections. It is simple and unbiased, but the outcome may differ if the election is re-run.

Weighted

Instead of choosing randomly, the algorithm looks at the next-choice preferences of voters who ranked the tied candidates. The candidate with the least downstream support is eliminated. This produces more deterministic results and factors in the full depth of voter preferences.
