Voter Prompts

Add custom questions that voters answer before casting their vote. For example, you could ask voters for their department, age group, or any other category.

Supported prompt types

  • Dropdown — voter picks from a list of options you define
  • Text — voter types a free-form response
  • Checkbox — voter checks a yes/no box

Filtering results

After voting closes, the ballot owner can filter and group results by voter responses — for example, viewing how each department voted separately.

Prompts are defined on the ballot management page after creation.
